# Vytal Tek Privacy Policy (publish draft)

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

This draft is intended for publishing at the URL configured as `VYTAL_PRIVACY_URL`
(default GitHub Pages `…/VytalTek/privacy/`). Replace with counsel-reviewed text before store submission.

## What we collect
- Account identifiers you provide
- Subscription and purchase status from Apple / Google (verified server-side)
- Health and wearable metrics you choose to sync (never fabricated)
- Device diagnostics needed to operate the app

## How we use data
- Provide App-Only and Connected Mode experiences
- Verify subscriptions before unlocking premium features
- Improve reliability and support

## Sharing
We do not sell personal health data. Store billing is handled by Apple and Google.
Service providers process data only to operate Vytal Tek.

## Your choices
- Disconnect wearables, disable demo mode, and manage permissions in Settings
- Cancel subscriptions in App Store / Play subscription management
